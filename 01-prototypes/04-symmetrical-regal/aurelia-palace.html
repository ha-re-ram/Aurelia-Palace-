01 · The Resort

A palace address in the heart of Heritage Valley

Set on 100 Aurelia Boulevard, near Aurelia Valley on Royal Highway in Aurelia Valley, Heritage District — Aurelia Palace is Royal Heritage Valley's private answer to the grand Indian hotel: ivory facades, antique gold detailing, and service that remembers your name.

Begin the morning beside the infinity pool, host the afternoon on our celebration lawns, and dance till late in our air-conditioned banquet halls — all under one roof, with full power backup, elevator access, room service and valet parking quietly taking care of everything else.
