Why is the pillar-less ballroom significant for weddings?

Traditional banquet halls carry large concrete pillars every fifteen to twenty feet, blocking views of the stage, mandap and couple's entrance for up to 35% of guests. Aurelia Palace's pillar-less design uses an advanced wide-span architectural truss, giving completely uninterrupted views and 360-degree photography angles from every corner of the 7,500 sq ft hall.

How many guests can you accommodate?

The Grand Pillar-less Ballroom hosts up to 550 seated and 800 floating guests. The Celebration Lawns host up to 700 seated and 1,100 floating guests. Combined, we comfortably accommodate over 1,200 to 1,600 guests with indoor dining and open-air celebrations running side by side.

What are the check-in and check-out timings?

Standard check-in is 12:00 PM and check-out is 10:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out can be arranged on availability with a prior request to our 24×7 concierge desk on +1 (555) 789-2345.

What is special about the celebration lawn grass?

The lawns are laid with imported dwarf grass trimmed to golf-green precision. Unlike ordinary garden turf that turns soft or marks lehengas and tuxedos, ours is dense, firm and heels-friendly, with underground storm drains and full insect control.

Do you offer catering, and can we bring our own?

Our banquet kitchens prepare North Indian, Royal Awadhi, Rajasthani, Marwari, Continental and Oriental menus, with live counters available on the lawns. Call us to discuss specific family or community requirements for your date.
