Welcome to Aurelia Palace

Refined comfort, unforgettable occasions.

Set on 100 Aurelia Boulevard, near Aurelia Valley on NH 32 in Aurelia Valley, Heritage District, Aurelia Palace is Royal Heritage Valley's address for elegant stays and landmark celebrations. Whether it is a business trip, a family gathering, or the wedding of the year, our team delivers service with quiet precision.

Every stay is designed around ease — immaculate rooms, dependable power backup, lift access to every floor, and a front desk that answers on the first ring.

  • Sprawling lawns & air-conditioned banquet halls for over 1,000 guests
  • Infinity swimming pool with poolside lounging areas
  • Valet parking, housekeeping & round-the-clock room service
  • Full power backup and elevator access throughout the property
