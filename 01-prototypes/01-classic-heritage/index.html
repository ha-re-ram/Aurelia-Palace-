About Aurelia Palace

Aurelia Palace is Royal Heritage Valley's premier luxury resort, perfectly situated in the serene surroundings of Royal Valley, Heritage District. We offer an exquisite blend of modern luxury and warm hospitality, making us the ideal destination for both leisure travelers and special occasions.

With 61 elegantly appointed rooms, world-class amenities, and dedicated event spaces, Aurelia Palace is your perfect choice for relaxation, celebration, and unforgettable memories in Heritage Valley. Located near Aurelia Valley, we provide easy access while maintaining a tranquil atmosphere.
